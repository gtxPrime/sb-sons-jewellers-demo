Before you buy

A short guide to buying well.

General guidance on gold, diamonds and care. Ask our team in the showroom for details on any piece.

24K99.9% pureCoins and bars. Too soft for most jewellery.
22K91.6% (916)Traditional gold jewellery.
18K75% (750)Often used for diamond and studded pieces.
14K58.5% (585)Light, hard-wearing daily wear.

Read the hallmark. A BIS hallmarked piece carries the BIS mark, the purity grade (for example 22K916) and a 6-character HUID code. You can check the HUID on the BIS Care app. Ask for an itemised bill showing gold weight, making charges, stone charges and hallmark details.

CutHow well the stone is shaped. It drives the sparkle.
ColourGraded from D (colourless) down the alphabet.
ClarityHow few natural marks inside. Graded from FL to I.
CaratWeight. One carat is 200 milligrams.

Ask for the grading certificate of any solitaire, and compare all four Cs together. A larger stone is not always the better one.

Store apartKeep pieces in soft pouches so they do not scratch each other.
Wear lastPerfume, spray and cream first, jewellery after.
Clean gentlyMild soap, lukewarm water and a soft brush. Dry well.
SilverUse a soft polishing cloth for tarnish and keep it dry.

Avoid chlorine, harsh cleaners and wearing delicate pieces while swimming, cooking or working out.
